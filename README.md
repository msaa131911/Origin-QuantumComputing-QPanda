# Origin — Quantum Computing with QPanda

> **Exploring Quantum Computing through QPanda, Mathematics, Algorithms, and Practical Experiments.**

Welcome to **Origin — QuantumComputing-QPanda**, a personal learning and experimentation repository dedicated to exploring the foundations and practical implementation of **Quantum Computing** using **QPanda**.

This repository represents my ongoing journey from fundamental quantum concepts to practical quantum programming, with a focus on understanding how quantum principles can be translated into algorithms and executable programs.

My long-term goal is to explore the intersection of:

* Quantum Computing
* Quantum Machine Learning
* Artificial Intelligence
* Mathematics
* Computer Science
* Quantum Algorithms

I am building my knowledge progressively through mathematical study, programming, experimentation, research-paper reading, and practical projects.

## Learning & Development Approach

The materials and implementations in this repository are developed as part of my personal learning journey. I study and learn from various **publicly available GitHub repositories, open-source projects, documentation, tutorials, and educational resources** related to quantum computing and QPanda.

I also use **Origin Quantum AI** and other AI-assisted tools as learning companions to help me understand concepts, explore implementations, debug code, and improve my programming knowledge.

The purpose of using these resources is not simply to copy existing work, but to **study, understand, experiment, modify, and gradually develop my own implementations and understanding**.

Where code, ideas, or resources are directly derived from external repositories or projects, I aim to respect their respective licenses and provide appropriate attribution.

This repository therefore serves as a **learning, experimentation, and documentation space** as I progress toward more advanced work in Quantum Computing and Quantum Machine Learning.
